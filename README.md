# Breakdowns-of-marketing-strategies-behind-top-brands

1. Redbull and their masterstroke in marketing!

How can anyone say MARKETING IS DEAD? How else do you manage to convince an entire generation that is so focused on health and fitness to consume a product that has mroe than 6 tablespoons of sugar in almost every can ? 
Other brands played along when consumers started questioning labels and ingredients, but Redbull decided to do something that not many comsumers have yet realised. 
Lets backtrack, what is Redbull?


While the world caled us healthy, Rebull focused on what that actually meant - a performacne driven and active generation.They used this insight to market their otherwise high in sugar drinks. We as consumers accepted this not because we are stupid, instead we just chose to look at benefits of consuming sugar - ENERGY
